P/01multi-agent

Research Assistant

Give it a research question. Four agents — planner, researcher, writer, critic — coordinate through a shared ChromaDB memory to produce a grounded report with citations. The critic loops until the writer's claims are backed by sources. Hit 0.90 groundedness on a 20-query RAGAS eval, roughly 2× the single-prompt baseline.

Workflow Platform

Automates inbound triage for a small team. Emails and form submissions land → a LangChain classifier tags intent and urgency → n8n routes structured responses to Gmail, Sheets, or Slack. ~70% less manual triage. Retry-safe webhooks with idempotency and schema-violation alerts.
